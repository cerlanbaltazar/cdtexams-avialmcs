Cadetship Program

Your Voyage Starts with One Exam.

Find out who can take the cadetship qualifying exam, what documents you'll need once you pass, and start the online exam for your department.

Cadetship Qualifying Exam For nautical students aged 25 and below
Step 1 · Before You Apply

To Take the Exam

Applicants must meet all three of the following.

01

Valid Google Email

A valid Google email account, which will be asked for during the registration process.

02

BSMT/BSME

Currently a student of a Nautical School, Institute, or University.

03

25 Years Old or Below

Must not exceed 25 years old within the year the exam was taken.

Step 2 · After You Pass

Cadetship Requirements

Other requirements if the examination is passed and you are selected to qualify for cadetship.

These documents are only needed once you've passed the exam and been selected. You don't need them to take the exam.

  1. a Transcript of RecordsTOR
  2. b Passport
  3. c Seaman's BookSIRB
  4. d Seaman's Identification DocumentSID
  5. e SRC
  6. f Medical CertificateReferral provided by company
  7. g BT with PSSR Upgraded COP
  8. h SDSD COP
  9. i Yellow Fever & Full COVID VaccinationBOQ Yellow Card issued
  10. j Other Pertinent RequirementsAs required by the manning agency
